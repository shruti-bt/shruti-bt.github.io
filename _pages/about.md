Hi there!

My name is Shruti Borkute-Thengane. I am a Senior Research Assistant at the Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD), Singapore, working under Dr Malika in the Multi-Agent Robotics Vision and Learning (MARVL) Lab.

I am currently working on automated, undocked ship hull assessment and inspection, enabling efficient pre-cleaning evaluation and post-cleaning verification to improve the speed, accuracy, and safety of maintenance operations.

In the hull inspection process, we focus on mapping critical niche areas—such as the propeller, rudder, sea chest, and bilge keel - both before and after cleaning, and assessing biofouling to quantify the extent of marine growth.

I completed my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Physics from [Gondwana University](https://unigug.ac.in/) (Gadchiroli, India). My Master's thesis was titled "Determination of Thermo-Acoustical Parameters of Aqueous Zinc Sulphate at Different Temperatures".
