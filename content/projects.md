## Autonomous Ship Hull Inspection
label: Autonomy · Planning · System integration
A modular hardware and software system that assesses a ship's hull before cleaning and inspects it afterwards, without dry-docking. I work on navigation, collision avoidance, trajectory planning and real-time control for the autonomous survey vessel, ROS 2 integration, and 3D reconstruction of the hull.
tag: Ongoing
tag: Poster, SMW 2026

## Language-Guided Robot Exploration
label: Vision-language models · Edge deployment
MERLION: a robot decides what to capture from a plain text prompt and returns enhanced, semantically aligned visual summaries. Pruning and quantisation let it run in real time on the robot's limited-memory hardware with minimal accuracy loss.
tag: ICRA 2025
tag: Patent filed
paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06953

## 3D Scene Graphs for Open-Ended Tasks
label: Embodied AI · Task execution
A 3D perception framework that switches between coarse and detailed scene representations as the task needs, with a cross-modal scene graph pipeline so a robot can carry out open-set tasks in real time.
tag: Under review

## Coral Health Monitoring in Murky Water
label: Field robotics · Human-robot collaboration
Murky-CHARM: an autonomous framework that maps, samples and assesses coral health in turbid water, with experts in the loop. It includes a murky-water coral segmentation dataset and a web-based annotation platform shared by scientists and citizen volunteers.
tag: Nature Communications 2026
tag: Patent filed
