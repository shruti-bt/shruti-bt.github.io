I build computer vision and machine learning systems that help robots **see and act in messy, real-world conditions**: murky water, poor light and constant motion.

Since 2023, I have been a Senior Research Assistant at the [MARVL Lab](https://www.malikameghjani.com/copy-of-home), SUTD, working with [Dr. Malika Meghjani](https://www.malikameghjani.com/), building embodied AI for underwater robots deployed in Singapore's waters. My work spans **vision-language models** that let a robot decide what to capture from a text prompt, **3D scene graphs** for open-ended task execution, **diffusion models** that restore murky imagery, and **edge AI**, using pruning and quantisation to run models in real time on board. I apply these to autonomous ship hull inspection and coral reef health monitoring.

Before SUTD, I applied machine learning to IoT systems at [United Arab Emirates University](https://www.uaeu.ac.ae/) and worked as a freelance data scientist. I hold a BSc in Physics, Mathematics and Computer Science and an MSc in Physics from [Gondwana University](https://unigug.ac.in/).
